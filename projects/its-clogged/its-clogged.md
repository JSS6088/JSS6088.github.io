# Its Clogged

_Its Clogged_ is a game where you play as a hotel janitor cleaning various bathrooms for the guests. As the story progresses, you will unlock more cleaning tools and face more difficult rooms to clean up.

## Technical Art {#tech-art}

As the technical artist, I created various shaders for the game's visual, all written in **Unity HLSL and C#**.
